**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, multi-domain):

Functional normality requires **all** of the following to hold sustainably for at least 30 consecutive days:
- **Direct military activity**: No US/Israeli strikes on Iranian territory or Iranian missile/drone attacks on Gulf shipping/US bases/allies. No active naval blockade or declared closure of Hormuz.
- **Proxy activity**: Significant restraint by Iran-backed groups (Houthis, Hezbollah remnants, PMF, etc.) with no major attacks on shipping or energy infrastructure.
- **Shipping security**: Strait of Hormuz and adjacent waters allow routine commercial transits with <5% attack/incident rate; US/coalition escorts minimal or phased out; AIS transparency restored.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Daily transits approach 70-80%+ of pre-war baseline (~50-60 vessels/day); full VLCC/LNG tanker flows resumed.
- **War-risk insurance**: Premiums return to low single-digit % or pre-war levels (~0.25-0.5% of hull value); major insurers (Lloyd’s, IG P&I Clubs) restore standard coverage without 72-hour cancellation clauses or government backstops.
- **Oil/LNG export continuity**: Gulf exports (Saudi, UAE, Iraq, Qatar, Kuwait, Iran) at 85%+ of pre-war volumes without force majeure declarations; no sustained pipeline bypass reliance.
- **Energy market stress**: Brent volatility < normal crisis levels; no prolonged spikes above $100-120/bbl attributable to Gulf disruption; Asian/European buyers resuming normal chartering.
- **Force posture**: Major actors (US, Iran, Gulf states) show verifiable drawdown—reduced US carrier presence, Iranian IRGC maritime assets stood down, no heightened alerts.
- **Absence of major escalation triggers**: No new Iranian nuclear breakthroughs, regime collapse risks, or proxy surges that could rapidly reverse gains.<grok:render card_id="8c085f" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">21</argument></grok:render>

A tactical pause, partial traffic, or lower prices alone do not qualify. Normalization is commercial/insurer-driven, not just military.
