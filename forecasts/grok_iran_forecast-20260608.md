**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, multi-domain):

Functional normality means:
- **Direct military activity**: No significant US/Israeli strikes on Iran or Iranian strikes on US/Israel/Gulf states (beyond very low-level incidents); no active combat operations.
- **Proxy activity**: Sustained restraint by Hezbollah, Houthis, Iraqi militias, etc., with no major attacks on shipping, Israel, or Gulf infrastructure.
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz/nearby waters**: Safe, routine commercial transits without routine attacks, mining threats, or selective tolls/extortion; US/Iranian naval forces not actively interdicting or blockading.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Vessel traffic and throughput approaching pre-war norms (~50-60+ tankers/commercial vessels daily; full oil/LNG export volumes restored).
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Premiums return to near pre-crisis levels (e.g., <<1% of hull value for transit); private market coverage widely available without government backstops.
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Gulf exports (Iran + Arab states) near pre-war levels (~20+ mb/d oil equivalent); no major force majeure or stranded cargoes; Asian/European buyers resuming normal offtake.
- **Energy market stress/volatility**: Brent and regional benchmarks stable with low volatility (no sustained spikes from chokepoint risk); no rationing or major supply shocks.
- **Force posture of major actors**: US naval presence de-escalated (not in blockade mode); Iranian IRGC navy not enforcing selective passage or threats; Gulf states resuming normal operations.
- **Absence of major new escalation triggers**: No major incidents (e.g., high-profile attacks, nuclear-related developments, leadership changes) that restart brinkmanship.
