**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, multi-domain):

Functional normality means observable, sustained restoration across these criteria, not merely a pause in fighting or temporary price relief:

- **Direct military activity**: No significant US/Israeli strikes on Iran or Iranian strikes on US/Israeli/Gulf targets; no active naval combat or mining in the Gulf.
- **Proxy activity**: Meaningful restraint by Hezbollah, Houthis, and Iraqi militias (no major attacks on shipping, Israel, or Gulf infrastructure).
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz and nearby waters**: Safe, routine commercial transits without routine IRGC coordination/tolls, attacks, or high-risk detours; no effective Iranian "management" veto.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Return to near pre-war volumes (~100+ vessels/day through Hormuz, full oil/LNG export flows).
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Standard commercial coverage available at near-pre-war premiums (not government-backed or extreme surcharges like 1-4%+ of hull value).
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Sustained exports from Gulf producers (including Iran) at or near pre-war levels without major disruptions or force majeure.
- **Energy market stress and volatility**: Oil prices stable without war premium; no shortages or rerouting crises.
- **Force posture of major actors**: US/Iranian drawdown from heightened alert; no dual blockades or massive forward deployments.
- **Absence of major new escalation triggers**: No unresolved flashpoints (e.g., Lebanon/Hezbollah full ceasefire with Israeli withdrawal, nuclear talks progress, no IRGC toll enforcement) likely to reignite disruption within the horizon.<grok:render card_id="3bce8e" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">0</argument></grok:render><grok:render card_id="d97404" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">2</argument></grok:render>
