**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, observable, multi-domain; not satisfied by temporary ceasefire, battlefield pause, or lower spot oil prices alone):

Functional normality requires **all** of the following sustained for at least 30 consecutive days without major reversal:
- **Direct military activity**: No US/Israeli strikes on Iranian territory or assets; no Iranian missile/drone attacks on US/Israeli/Gulf state targets or shipping; cessation of active kinetic exchanges.
- **Proxy activity**: Significant de-escalation in Hezbollah/Houthi/PMF operations tied to the Iran theater (e.g., no major rocket barrages or attacks on shipping).
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz and nearby waters**: No successful or attempted attacks on commercial vessels; freedom of navigation without effective Iranian veto, fees/tolls that function as protection rackets, or mandatory "Iranian management" contradicting international norms.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Daily transits approaching pre-war norms (~50-60+ vessels/day, including major tankers/LNG carriers) with consistent AIS data and voluntary commercial routing (not just escorted or dark transits).
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Premiums return to low single-digit multiples of pre-war levels (e.g., ~0.25-0.5% of hull value per transit or less) with broad private market availability, no widespread government backstopping required, and Joint War Committee delisting or major de-risking of the Gulf.
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Iranian and Gulf exports flowing at near pre-war volumes/trajectories without major force majeure declarations; no sustained chokepoint-induced global supply gaps.
- **Energy market stress and volatility**: Brent/WTI volatility normalizes (e.g., no persistent geopolitical risk premium driving sustained spikes or contango distortions beyond typical seasonality); Asian/European buyers resuming routine contracting without stockpiling panic.
- **Force posture of major actors**: US naval presence and Gulf state alerts de-escalate to routine levels; no ongoing blockades or heightened readiness for Hormuz closure.
- **Absence of major new escalation triggers**: No unresolved nuclear breakthroughs, leadership crises in Iran, or proxy flare-ups that credibly threaten the above within the horizon.
