**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, multi-domain):

Functional normality means observable restoration across these criteria simultaneously for a sustained period (e.g., 2–4 weeks without major reversal):

- **Direct military activity**: No kinetic strikes by US/Israel/Iran or allies in the Gulf region or on shipping; no active mining or major naval confrontations.
- **Proxy activity**: Significant restraint by Houthis, Hezbollah remnants, PMF, etc., with no sustained attacks on Gulf shipping, infrastructure, or US/Gulf forces.
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz and nearby waters**: Safe, unrestricted commercial transit without mandatory Iranian permits/insurance, IRGC harassment, or high-risk escorts; mine clearance verified by international standards.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Vessel traffic (tankers, LNG, containers) returns to ~70–80%+ of pre-war baselines (pre-Feb 28, 2026 levels of ~100+ vessels/day); no widespread rerouting around Cape or force majeure declarations.
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Premiums return to low single-digit multiples of pre-war (not 1000x+ or unavailable); private insurers resume broad coverage without government backstops as primary reliance.
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Gulf production/exports (including Iran) recover to ~70–90%+ of pre-war volumes; no major shut-ins or storage constraints; flows through Hormuz normalized.
- **Energy market stress and volatility**: Brent/WTI volatility normalizes (e.g., no sustained $10+/day swings from Gulf risk); prices reflect fundamentals without war premium; Asian/European buyers resume routine contracting.
- **Force posture of major actors**: US/coalition drawdown of surge assets; Iranian IRGC naval forces return to routine (not heightened alert/control); no blockades.
- **Absence of major new escalation triggers**: No disputes over nuclear program derailing the MoU timeline, no major proxy flare-ups, no unresolved de-mining or channel control issues.

Temporary pauses, partial traffic, subsidized insurance, or lower prices alone do **not** qualify.
