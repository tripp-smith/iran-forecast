**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, observable, multi-domain):

Functional normality requires **all** of the following sustained for at least 30 consecutive days without major reversal:

- **Direct military activity**: No US/Israeli strikes on Iranian territory or Iranian missile/drone strikes on US/Israeli/Gulf targets at scale (>5 significant incidents/month).
- **Proxy activity**: Significant restraint by Hezbollah, Houthis, Iraqi PMF, etc. — attacks on shipping/Gulf states/Israel reduced >80% from peak.
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz**: No routine attacks/seizures/mining; US/Iran/Gulf naval forces not in direct confrontation; freedom of navigation effectively restored for neutral flagged vessels.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Daily tanker/LNG transits ≥70% of 2025 pre-war baseline (~20+ mb/d oil equivalent throughput); stranded vessel backlog cleared.
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Private market premiums back to <0.5-1% of hull value per transit (vs. current 1-5%+ or uninsurable); no widespread government backstops or cancellations.
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Gulf producers (Saudi, UAE, Iraq, Qatar, Kuwait, Iran) exporting at ≥85% of pre-war capacity without forced rationing or major rerouting; no sustained global supply shock.
- **Energy market stress**: Brent volatility <25% annualized; no persistent >$20/bbl risk premium; Asian/European buyers resuming normal contracting without panic stockpiling.
- **Force posture**: Major drawdown or de-escalation of US carrier groups, Israeli alert levels, IRGC naval deployments; no ongoing blockade/enforcement actions.
- **Absence of major new escalation triggers**: No nuclear breakout by Iran, no major leadership assassination, no proxy surge, no new minefields/anti-ship missile barrages.

A temporary ceasefire or reduced violence alone does **not** qualify.
