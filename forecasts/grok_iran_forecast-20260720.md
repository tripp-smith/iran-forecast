**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, observable, multi-domain): 

Return to functional normality requires *all* of the following sustained for at least 30 consecutive days without major reversal:
- **Direct military activity**: No US/Iran strikes on each other’s forces/assets or Gulf infrastructure; no Iranian attempts to close or mine the Strait.
- **Proxy activity**: Significant restraint by Iran-backed groups (Houthis, Iraqi militias, Hezbollah remnants) targeting shipping, Gulf states, or energy infrastructure; no major new activations.
- **Shipping security**: Safe commercial transit through Strait of Hormuz and adjacent waters with minimal attacks/incidents; US/coalition naval presence reduced to routine patrols, not active combat/blockade enforcement.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Vessel traffic (AIS + satellite-derived) at ≥80% of pre-war baseline (typically ~100-130+ ships/day through Hormuz); consistent inbound/outbound flows without mass rerouting or "dark" fleet dominance.<grok:render card_id="345ecb" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">13</argument></grok:render><grok:render card_id="0103c9" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">27</argument></grok:render>
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Premiums return to low single-digit multiples of pre-war levels (e.g., <0.5-1% of hull value per transit vs. current spikes to 1-5% or 4,000x in extremes); broad availability without convoy requirements.<grok:render card_id="081063" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">14</argument></grok:render><grok:render card_id="472508" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">15</argument></grok:render>
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Gulf exports (Saudi, UAE, Iraq, Qatar, Iran) at ≥90% pre-war volumes; no sustained storage constraints or forced rerouting via pipelines/alternatives.
- **Energy market stress/volatility**: Brent/WTI volatility and risk premia normalized (e.g., no persistent $10+/bbl war premium); Asian/European buyers resuming standard contracts without hedging spikes.
- **Force posture**: Major drawdown of US/UK naval/air assets from high-alert surge; Iran IRGC Navy not in forward disruptive posture; Gulf states de-alerting.
- **Absence of major new escalation triggers**: No significant incidents (e.g., tanker strikes, infrastructure attacks, high-casualty events) that restart cycles; credible diplomatic track toward durable de-escalation.

A temporary pause, lower prices from demand destruction, or partial traffic recovery does **not** count. Full commercial normalization lags military pauses substantially due to insurer/buyer caution and residual risks.<grok:render card_id="217d65" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">18</argument></grok:render>
