**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, multi-domain):

Functional normality means observable restoration of pre-war (pre-Feb 28, 2026) commercial and security conditions across key indicators, sustained for at least 30 days without major reversal. It requires:

- **Direct military activity**: Near-zero kinetic strikes, drone/missile attacks, or naval engagements between Iran, US, Israel, and Gulf states in/near the Gulf.
- **Proxy activity**: Significant restraint by Iran-backed groups (e.g., Houthis, others) with no sustained attacks on shipping or infrastructure.
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz/nearby waters**: Safe, unrestricted transit without IRGC "tolls," selective approvals, mines, or harassment; US/Iran blockades lifted; freedom of navigation restored.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Daily transits approaching pre-war ~60+ vessels/day (normal ~20M bpd oil equivalent capacity); no mass stranding or rerouting.
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Premiums return to low single-digit multiples of pre-war levels (e.g., ~0.1-0.25% of hull value for 7 days, not 1-10%+); broad private market availability without government backstops dominating.
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Gulf exports (Saudi, UAE, Qatar, Iraq, Iran) at or near pre-war volumes via Hormuz; no force majeure declarations or major storage crises.
- **Energy market stress/volatility**: Brent and regional benchmarks stable near long-term averages (e.g., $70-90/bbl range without war premium spikes); no rationing or major supply shocks.
- **Force posture of major actors**: US naval presence reduced to routine patrols; Iranian IRGC Navy not enforcing "management" or new traffic schemes; no heightened alerts.
- **Absence of major new escalation triggers**: No breakthroughs in nuclear program, major proxy flare-ups, or infrastructure attacks that could rapidly reverse gains.
