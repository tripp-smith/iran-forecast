**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, multi-domain): 

Functional normality means a stable, commercially viable operating environment in the Persian Gulf/Strait of Hormuz approximating pre-Feb 28, 2026 conditions, without equating temporary pauses or price relief to recovery. It requires **all** of the following sustained for at least 30 days:

- **Direct military activity**: No kinetic strikes, mine-laying, or drone/missile attacks by state or proxy forces on shipping, infrastructure, or military assets in/near the Gulf.
- **Proxy activity**: Significant restraint by Iran-backed groups (e.g., Houthis, others) with no sustained disruption to Gulf or adjacent shipping/energy targets.
- **Shipping security**: Safe, predictable transit through the Strait of Hormuz without mandatory Iranian "fees/tolls," IRGC-permitted routes only, or selective approvals; freedom of navigation respected in international waters.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Vessel traffic and throughput returning to ≥70-80% of pre-war norms (~100-140 vessels/day, full oil/LNG export capacity).
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Premiums and availability normalizing to near pre-war levels (e.g., <<1% of hull value per transit, broad private market coverage without government backstops dominating).
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Persian Gulf producers exporting near full pre-war volumes (~20+ mbpd oil equivalent) without major rerouting, storage constraints, or buyer hesitancy.
- **Energy market stress/volatility**: Oil prices stable without war premium; no rationing/shortages in Asia/Europe; low implied volatility.
- **Force posture**: Drawdown of heightened US/coalition naval presence, Iranian IRGC navy restraint, no major new deployments.
- **Absence of major escalation triggers**: No unresolved disputes over Hormuz sovereignty/control, nuclear issues, or Lebanon that credibly risk renewed closure; credible diplomatic mechanisms in place.

A temporary ceasefire, partial traffic resumption, or oil price drop alone does **not** qualify.
