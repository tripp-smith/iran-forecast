**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, observable, multi-domain):

Functional normality means the Persian Gulf operates as a high-volume, low-risk commercial chokepoint with minimal friction, comparable to pre-2026 baselines (e.g., ~100-140 daily transits through Hormuz, stable ~20M bpd oil flows). It requires **all** of the following sustained for at least 30 consecutive days without major reversal:

- **Direct military activity**: No US/Israeli strikes on Iranian territory/assets or Iranian missile/drone attacks on US/Israeli/Gulf targets. No active naval engagements or mining.
- **Proxy activity**: Restraint or significant de-escalation by Hezbollah, Houthis, Iraqi militias (e.g., no sustained attacks on shipping, Israel, or Gulf infrastructure).
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz and nearby waters**: No attacks on commercial vessels; freedom of navigation without mandatory Iranian coordination, US interdiction, or widespread AIS-dark routing. Risk levels allow standard commercial routing.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Return to ~80%+ of pre-war daily transits (~80-110+ vessels/day); no widespread rerouting around Africa or avoidance.
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: War-risk premiums normalized (near pre-war levels, not elevated "additional premiums" or exclusions for Hormuz/Gulf); insurers willing to cover standard hull/cargo without special riders.
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Iranian and Gulf exports at or near pre-war volumes (~2M+ bpd Iranian shadow + regular); no sustained blockades or forced reductions impacting global supply.
- **Energy market stress and volatility**: Brent/WTI volatility index normalized (e.g., no sustained >$10-15/bbl spikes or fear-driven contango from Gulf risk); Asian/European buyers resuming normal contracting without premiums for alternative sourcing.
- **Force posture of major actors**: US naval presence returns to routine deterrence (not active blockade/enforcement); Iranian IRGC Navy/ proxies not postured for closure or harassment; Gulf states not on high alert.
- **Absence of major new escalation triggers**: No nuclear breakthroughs, leadership assassinations, major proxy escalations, or failed high-level talks that restart direct/proxy cycles.
