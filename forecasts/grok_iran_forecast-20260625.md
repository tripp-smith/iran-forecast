**Step 1: Define the forecast target**

“Return to functional normality” in the Persian Gulf is defined operationally as the point where multiple observable conditions are met simultaneously and sustainably (not a temporary lull or ceasefire paper agreement):

- **Direct military activity**: No kinetic strikes, mine-laying, or naval harassment by state actors in/near the Strait of Hormuz or Gulf waters.
- **Proxy activity**: No significant attacks or credible threats from Iranian-backed groups (e.g., Houthis, remaining Hezbollah elements, Iraqi militias) targeting shipping, energy infrastructure, or Gulf states.
- **Shipping security**: Consistent safe commercial transit through the Strait without escorts, permits/tolls imposed by Iran beyond international norms, or credible threats; threat levels downgraded to low/moderate by bodies like JMIC.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Daily vessel transits approaching or exceeding ~70-80% of pre-war norms (~100+ vessels/day vs. historical ~130-140).
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Premiums return to near pre-war levels (<<1% of hull value) with broad private market availability; no reliance on government backstops or extreme surcharges.
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Full resumption of Gulf exports (Iran, Saudi, UAE, Qatar, etc.) at near pre-war volumes without force majeure declarations or major rerouting; stable loading/operations at key terminals.
- **Energy market stress and volatility**: Brent and regional benchmarks stabilize without war premium; no sustained spikes >$10-15/bbl attributable to Gulf risks; Asian/European buyers resume normal contracting.
- **Force posture of major actors**: US/coalition naval presence reduced to routine patrols (not blockade/escort-heavy); Iranian IRGC Navy not posturing for closure or sovereignty enforcement beyond norms; no heightened alerts.
- **Absence of major new escalation triggers**: No disputes over nuclear compliance, sanctions relief implementation, or proxy flare-ups that credibly threaten the above within the horizon.

Normality requires commercial confidence and behavior, not just military de-escalation.
