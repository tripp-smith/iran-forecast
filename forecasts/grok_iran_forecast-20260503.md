**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, observable, multi-domain):

Functional normality means:
- **Direct military activity**: No kinetic strikes (missiles, drones, naval clashes) between Iran/US/Israel or their forces in/near the Gulf for ≥30 consecutive days.
- **Proxy activity**: Significant restraint (≥70% reduction from peak) by Hezbollah, Houthis, PMF, etc., with no major Gulf-targeted attacks.
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz/nearby waters**: Sustained safe commercial transit (no attacks/mining on commercial vessels); US/Iran blockades lifted or ineffective; routine naval escorts unnecessary.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Daily transits ≥70-80% of pre-war baseline (~100-140 vessels/day); backlog of stranded ships (~800-2,000) largely cleared.
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Premiums return to low single-digit multiples of pre-war (e.g., <1% hull value vs. current 1-5%+ or cancellations); full private market coverage available without government backstops.
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Gulf exports (Saudi, UAE, Iraq, Qatar, etc.) at ≥85% pre-war levels; Iranian exports normalized without selective tolls/blockades; no widespread force majeure.
- **Energy market stress/volatility**: Brent/WTI volatility index normalized (no sustained >$100/bbl spikes or rationing); Asian/European buyers resuming standard contracts.
- **Force posture of major actors**: US/Coalition drawdown from heightened alert; Iran/IRGC not posturing for closure; Gulf states resuming routine operations.
- **Absence of major new escalation triggers**: No unresolved nuclear/missile issues, leadership assassinations, or proxy flare-ups driving immediate risk premia.
