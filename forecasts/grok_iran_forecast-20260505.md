**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, observable, multi-domain):

Functional normality means all these must hold simultaneously for a sustained period (at least 2–4 weeks without reversal):

- **Direct military activity**: No strikes, exchanges of fire, or attacks by US/Israel/Iran or their forces in/near the Gulf (including Hormuz) or against Gulf state targets.
- **Proxy activity**: No significant Houthi/Hezbollah/PMF/IRGC-QF attacks on shipping, ports, or energy infrastructure traceable to Iranian direction.
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz and nearby waters**: Safe commercial transit without routine escorts, attacks, mines, or credible threats; AIS/vessel tracking shows consistent traffic.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Vessel traffic and tanker/LNG carrier movements return to ≥70–80% of pre-Feb 2026 baselines (~3,000 vessels/month pre-war).
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Private market premiums normalize to low-single-digit multiples of pre-war levels (e.g., <1% of hull value per transit vs. current 1–5%+ or cancellations); no widespread government backstops or notices of cancellation required.
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Persian Gulf producers (Saudi, UAE, Qatar, Iraq, Kuwait, Iran) export at or near pre-war volumes without major storage buildup or forced rerouting; no prolonged halts.
- **Energy market stress and volatility**: Brent/WTI and JKM/TTF benchmarks stabilize without war-premium spikes (>20–30% volatility attributable to Gulf risk); no emergency SPR releases or rerouting dominating flows.
- **Force posture of major actors**: US/UK/coalition drawdown from heightened escort/blockade posture; Iranian naval/IRGC forces not actively contesting transit; no dual blockades.
- **Absence of major new escalation triggers**: No nuclear breakthroughs, leadership changes sparking instability, or proxy surges; sustained diplomatic track (e.g., extended ceasefire with enforcement).

A temporary pause, lower prices from demand destruction, or partial escorted transits do **not** qualify.
