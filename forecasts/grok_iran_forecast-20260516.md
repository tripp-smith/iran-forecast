**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, multi-domain; not met by temporary ceasefire, battlefield pause, or lower spot prices alone):

- **Direct military activity**: No kinetic strikes, mine-laying, or harassment by state forces (Iran, US, Israel, proxies) in/near Gulf for ≥30 consecutive days.
- **Proxy activity**: No sustained attacks or credible threats from Houthis, PMF, Hezbollah remnants, or IRGC-linked groups targeting Gulf shipping/assets.
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz/nearby waters**: Safe commercial transit without selective Iranian approvals, tolls/fees violating international norms, or routine self-declared nationality routing for protection; vessel traffic ≥80% of pre-war baseline (~138 vessels/day).<grok:render card_id="7a054d" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">0</argument></grok:render>
- **Commercial transit levels**: Sustained high-volume tanker/LNG movements without rerouting around Africa or significant loitering/avoidance.
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Premiums return to near pre-war levels (<<1% of hull value); full private-market coverage available without government backstops or voyage-by-voyage repricing.
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Gulf producers (Saudi, UAE, Qatar, Iraq, Kuwait, Iran) export at ≥90% pre-war rates via Hormuz without force majeure, storage overflows, or production cuts.
- **Energy market stress/volatility**: Brent/WTI volatility normalizes; no rationing/shortages in Asia/Europe; no sustained >$20–30/bbl risk premium.
- **Force posture of major actors**: US/Gulf naval/air presence de-escalates to routine deterrence (no blockades, heightened alerts, or major deployments); Iran does not maintain “management” regime over strait.
- **Absence of major new escalation triggers**: No nuclear breakthroughs, proxy flare-ups, or unresolved sovereignty/blockade disputes that could rapidly reverse gains.<grok:render card_id="9a45c4" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">1</argument></grok:render>
