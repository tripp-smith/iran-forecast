**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, observable, multi-domain):

- **Direct military activity**: No kinetic strikes (missiles, drones, naval clashes) between Iran/US/Israel or their forces in/near the Gulf for at least 30 consecutive days; no active blockades.
- **Proxy activity**: Significant restraint or de-escalation by Hezbollah, Houthis, PMF/IRI groups—no sustained attacks on shipping, Gulf infrastructure, or Israel/US assets traceable to Iranian direction.
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz and nearby waters**: Safe commercial transit without routine attacks, mining incidents, or seizures; vessel traffic approaching pre-war daily averages (~100-140 transits/day); clearance of mines/UXO largely complete or managed.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Sustained recovery to ≥70-80% of pre-war volumes without major rerouting or avoidance.
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Premiums return to low single-digit multiples of pre-war levels (e.g., <<1% of hull value per transit, not 1-5% or 4000x spikes); standard private coverage widely available without government backstops dominating.
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Persian Gulf exporters (Saudi, UAE, Iraq, Qatar, Iran) operating near capacity with minimal forced rerouting (e.g., via Yanbu); no prolonged outages from damage.
- **Energy market stress and volatility**: Brent/WTI volatility indices normalized (no sustained extreme swings); no ongoing supply shocks requiring IEA emergency releases; prices reflect fundamentals more than risk premium.
- **Force posture of major actors**: US drawdown or stabilization of elevated presence; no Iranian mass mobilization or mine-laying; Gulf states not on heightened alert.
