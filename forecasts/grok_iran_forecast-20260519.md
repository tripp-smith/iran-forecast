**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, observable, multi-domain): Functional normality requires *all* of the following to hold simultaneously and sustainably for at least 30 consecutive days:

- **Direct military activity**: No kinetic strikes (missiles, drones, naval engagements) between US/Israel/Gulf states and Iran or its forces in/near the Gulf; no US strikes on Iranian territory or vice versa.
- **Proxy activity**: No significant new proxy attacks (Hezbollah, Houthis, PMF/Islamic Resistance in Iraq) on shipping, Gulf infrastructure, Israel, or US forces attributable to Iranian direction or coordination.
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz and nearby waters**: No attacks on commercial vessels; freedom of navigation without selective Iranian tolls/permissions or US interdictions; AIS-visible transits return to ≥70% of pre-war baseline (~50-60+ vessels/day).
- **Commercial transit levels**: Tanker and commercial traffic through Hormuz at ≥60-70% of normal pre-war volumes; stranded vessels (hundreds reported) largely cleared; no widespread rerouting or avoidance.
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Premiums fall to low single-digit multiples of pre-war levels (e.g., <1% of hull value vs. current ~5%+); major insurers resume coverage without exclusions for the Gulf/Hormuz route.
