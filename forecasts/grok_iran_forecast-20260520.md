**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, observable, multi-domain): 

Return to functional normality requires *all* of the following sustained for at least 30 consecutive days without major reversal:

- **Direct military activity**: No strikes by US/Israel on Iranian territory or vice versa; US naval blockade effectively lifted or minimized to sanctions enforcement only.
- **Proxy activity**: Significant restraint by Hezbollah, Houthis, Iraqi militias (e.g., attacks on shipping, Israel, Gulf states, or US forces drop to pre-2025 baseline levels or lower).
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz and nearby waters**: Daily transits return to ≥70% of pre-war average (~100+ vessels/day); attacks/incidents near zero; no active mining or credible threats forcing rerouting.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Tanker traffic and non-energy shipping recover to ≥60-70% of normal; stranded vessels (currently ~600-2000+ inside Gulf) largely cleared without major losses.
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Premiums fall to ≤0.5% of hull value per transit (from current elevated 1-3%+ or unavailable levels); full commercial coverage widely available without government backstops.
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Persian Gulf exports (Saudi, UAE, Iraq, Kuwait, Qatar, Iran) recover to within 20% of pre-war levels (~17-20+ mb/d combined crude/condensate equivalent); no widespread force majeure or shut-ins.
- **Energy market stress and volatility**: Brent/WTI volatility normalizes (e.g., <30-40% annualized); no sustained premiums from chokepoint risk; Asian/European buyers resume normal contracting without major inventory draws or demand destruction.
- **Force posture of major actors**: US Navy presence returns to routine patrol levels (not blockade/enforcement); Iranian IRGC Navy/ proxies de-escalate patrols; Gulf states resume normal port/refinery operations.
