**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, observable, multi-domain):<grok:render card_id="7b3317" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">1</argument></grok:render><grok:render card_id="82bbb4" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">42</argument></grok:render>

Functional normality means:
- **Direct military activity**: No sustained US/Israeli strikes on Iranian targets or Iranian missile/drone attacks on Gulf shipping, bases, or allies (sporadic incidents below escalation threshold acceptable if contained).
- **Proxy activity**: Restraint or significant de-escalation by Houthis, Hezbollah, Iraqi militias (no major attacks disrupting shipping or energy infrastructure).
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz/nearby waters**: Routine safe transit without routine attacks, mines, or credible threats; daily transits approaching pre-war ~100-180 vessels.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Tanker and commercial traffic at or near pre-war volumes; minimal stranded vessels; operators routing normally without major detours.
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Premiums back to low single-digit % of hull value (near pre-war ~0.25-1-3%); full private coverage available without government backstops.
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Gulf exports (Iran + others) at ~80-100% pre-war levels (~20-25M bpd oil equivalent); no force majeure declarations; stable flows.
- **Energy market stress/volatility**: Brent crude near pre-war levels (~$70-80/bbl) with low volatility; no sustained spikes from Gulf disruptions.
- **Force posture of major actors**: US/Israeli drawdown from high-alert; Iranian naval/IRGC reduced asymmetric posture; no active blockade.
- **Absence of major new escalation triggers**: No major attacks on shipping, leadership strikes, or proxy surges; credible diplomatic track or tacit understandings holding.

A temporary ceasefire or battlefield pause without commercial/insurance normalization does **not** count. Lower oil prices alone (e.g., via alternative supply) do not suffice if chokepoint risks persist.<grok:render card_id="06f496" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">52</argument></grok:render>

Intermittent flare-ups with partial lulls; gradual traffic/insurance recovery by 6-12 months via US/Gulf escorts and Iranian restraint under pressure; full normality by ~12-18 months barring major shocks.

Rapid diplomatic breakthrough (e.g., Hormuz guarantees + sanctions relief); US naval guarantees restore confidence quickly; proxies stand down. (Upside ~+20-30% to probs.)

### Prolonged Instability Case
Iranian proxies + mines sustain threats; insurance stays elevated; repeated escalations. Strongest case: Enduring Iranian asymmetric leverage + political will gaps.
