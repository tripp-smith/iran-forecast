**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, observable, multi-domain):

Functional normality means **sustained, commercially viable operations approaching pre-war (pre-Feb 28, 2026) baselines** without reliance on ad-hoc military escorts, state backstops, or belligerent permission regimes, across:

- **Direct military activity**: No strikes, mine-laying, or kinetic incidents by state or proxy forces in/near the Gulf/Hormuz.
- **Proxy activity**: Restraint by Iranian-backed groups (e.g., Houthis, others) targeting shipping/energy.
- **Shipping security**: Safe, routine transit without Iranian "permission/permit/insurance" mandates, dark transits, or frequent interceptions; freedom of navigation per international norms.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Daily transits near pre-war ~100-140 vessels (with ~20% global oil/ significant LNG); no major stranded fleets or rerouting via Cape.
- **War-risk insurance**: Premiums back to low single-digit basis points (e.g., ~0.1-0.25% of hull value per transit); standard commercial coverage widely available without government backstops.
- **Oil/LNG export continuity**: Full resumption of ~20% global flows through Hormuz at market-driven volumes; no force majeure or significant stranded cargoes.
- **Energy market stress/volatility**: Oil/LNG prices and volatility normalized (no sustained war premium); no major supply shortfalls.
- **Force posture**: Drawdown of heightened US/Iranian naval/air presence; no dual blockades or persistent high-alert postures.
- **Absence of major escalation triggers**: No active disputes over nuclear program, sanctions, Lebanon, or Hormuz administration that risk rapid reversion.

A temporary pause/ceasefire or partial traffic increase does **not** qualify.
