**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”**<grok:render card_id="5b8a5b" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">10</argument></grok:render><grok:render card_id="1bf1ce" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">13</argument></grok:render>

Functional normality means observable, sustained multi-domain conditions where:

- **Direct military activity**: No US/Iran/Israel strikes, missile/drone exchanges, or naval clashes in/near the Gulf for ≥14 consecutive days.
- **Proxy activity**: No significant Houthi/Hezbollah/PMF attacks on shipping, Gulf states, or US assets traceable to Iranian direction.
- **Shipping security**: Strait of Hormuz and adjacent waters allow unrestricted commercial transit without routine seizures, attacks, or mandatory naval escorts; AIS data shows ≥80-100 daily transits (near pre-war ~130 average).
- **Commercial transit levels**: Tanker and cargo volumes recover to ≥70-80% of pre-war baselines; no widespread rerouting via Cape of Good Hope.
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Premiums fall to ≤0.5% of hull value per transit (from current elevated 1-6%+ peaks); standard P&I clubs restore full coverage without exclusions or government reinsurance backstops.
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Gulf producers (Saudi, UAE, Iraq, Qatar, Iran) export near pre-war rates (~20+ mbpd oil equivalent) with minimal force majeure declarations; no major storage overflows or production curtailments.
- **Energy market stress and volatility**: Brent volatility <25% annualized; no sustained premiums >$10-15/bbl above global benchmarks attributable to Gulf risk.
- **Force posture**: US/Iranian naval/air assets de-escalate (reduced blockades, patrols, high-alert status); no major forward deployments or threats of closure.
- **Absence of major new escalation triggers**: No breakthroughs in nuclear talks or proxy flare-ups that credibly threaten the above within the horizon.

A temporary pause, partial transits, or lower prices alone do not qualify. All elements must align for ≥2-4 weeks to count as "returned."
