**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (observable, multi-domain):

Functional normality requires *all* of the following sustained for at least 14 consecutive days without major reversal:
- **Direct military activity**: No strikes by US/Israel on Iranian territory or Iranian ballistic/cruise/drone attacks on US/Israeli/Gulf targets above sporadic low-level incidents.
- **Proxy activity**: Meaningful restraint by Hezbollah, Houthis, Iraqi PMF, etc. (attacks down >80% from peak and no major new campaigns).
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz and nearby waters**: No routine Iranian interdiction, mining, or attacks on commercial vessels; US/Gulf naval escorts optional but not required for most traffic; AIS/transponder data shows free passage.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Vessel traffic through Hormuz at ≥70% of pre-war baseline (~100-140 commercial transits/day); no significant stranding/backlog of >500 vessels.
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Lloyd’s/JWC war-risk premiums for Gulf/Hormuz routes return to near pre-war levels (low single-digit surcharges, not 10-100x spikes or uninsurable).
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Gulf exporters (Saudi, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Iraq, Iran) shipping at ≥80% of pre-war combined volumes; no sustained floating storage crisis or refinery/port shutdowns due to threats.
- **Energy market stress and volatility**: Brent/WTI volatility index back to normal ranges (<30-40 annualized); no persistent supply shock premiums (> $10-15/bbl risk premium); Asian/European buyers resuming normal contracting without panic diversion.
- **Force posture of major actors**: US/Israeli naval/air assets in region at routine deployment levels (not surge); Iranian IRGC Navy not in heightened alert/blockade mode; Gulf states not in emergency import mode.
- **Absence of major new escalation triggers**: No imminent nuclear breakout signals, leadership decapitation risks, or proxy activation thresholds crossed.

A temporary ceasefire, reduced attacks, or lower spot prices alone do **not** suffice.
