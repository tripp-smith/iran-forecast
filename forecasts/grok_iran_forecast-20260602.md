**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”**

“Return to functional normality” requires **all** of the following observable conditions to hold **simultaneously and sustainably** (not just a pause or partial recovery):

- **Direct military activity**: No kinetic strikes (missiles, drones, naval clashes) between US/Israel and Iran or their forces in/near the Gulf for at least 30 consecutive days.
- **Proxy activity**: No significant attacks or credible threats from Iranian proxies (e.g., Houthis, Hezbollah remnants, Shia militias) targeting Gulf shipping, energy infrastructure, or US/Gulf forces.
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz and nearby waters**: Safe, unhindered commercial transit without routine naval escorts, mine threats, or attacks; no active dual blockades; AIS/transponder use normalized.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Daily tanker and commercial vessel transits return to ≥70-80% of pre-war baselines (~100-150+ vessels/day through Hormuz).
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Private market war-risk premiums return to near pre-war levels (<0.5% of hull value per transit); full P&I coverage widely available without government backstops.
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Iranian, Saudi, UAE, and other Gulf exports flowing at or near pre-war volumes; no significant stranded tankers or production curtailments due to security.
- **Energy market stress and volatility**: Brent/WTI volatility and risk premiums normalize (e.g., no sustained geopolitical premium >$10-15/bbl); no fuel shortages in Asia/Europe.
- **Force posture of major actors**: US naval presence returns to routine rotation (not heightened blockade/escort mode); Iranian IRGC naval forces de-escalate mine-laying/threat posture; no major new deployments.
- **Absence of major new escalation triggers**: No unresolved nuclear breakout concerns, leadership instability in Tehran driving adventurism, or proxy flare-ups that could rapidly reverse gains.

A temporary ceasefire, lower (but still elevated) oil prices, or partial escorted transits do **not** qualify.
