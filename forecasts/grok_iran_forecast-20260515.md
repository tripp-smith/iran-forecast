**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, observable, multi-domain):

Functional normality requires **all** of the following sustained for at least 30 consecutive days without major reversal:

- **Direct military activity**: No kinetic strikes (missiles, drones, naval clashes) between Iran/US/Israel/Gulf states or their forces in/near the Gulf; no active US naval blockade of Iranian ports or Iranian interdiction of third-party shipping.
- **Proxy activity**: No significant attacks by Iran-aligned groups (Houthis, Iraqi militias, etc.) on Gulf shipping, infrastructure, or US/Gulf forces.
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz and nearby waters**: Routine commercial transits without mandatory Iranian vetting/fees, selective approvals, or credible threats; AIS data shows >70-80% of pre-war daily average (~100-150+ transits/day); mines cleared or neutralized; no routine warship escorts required for most traffic.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Tanker and commercial vessel traffic at ≥70% of pre-war baseline; no widespread rerouting via pipelines/overland as primary method.
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Standard commercial hull/cargo war-risk premiums return to low single-digit multiples of pre-war levels (e.g., <0.5% of hull value per transit vs. current elevated 1-3%); broad market capacity available without government backstops or exclusions for Persian Gulf.
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Persian Gulf producers (Saudi, UAE, Iraq, Qatar, etc.) exporting at ≥80% of pre-war rates through the Strait without force majeure declarations or major storage backups; Iranian exports normalized subject to sanctions.
- **Energy market stress and volatility**: Brent/WTI volatility index normalized (no sustained spikes from chokepoint risk); no rationing or acute shortages in Asia/Europe; futures curves not in deep backwardation from disruption fears.
- **Force posture of major actors**: US/UK/France/coalition drawdown from heightened escort/blockade posture; Iranian IRGC Navy/ashore forces not postured for sustained interdiction; no major new deployments.
- **Absence of major new escalation triggers**: No credible threats to close Hormuz, nuclear breakout moves, or leadership decapitation risks; diplomatic framework (even imperfect) reducing immediate flashpoints.

A temporary pause, selective Iranian-approved transits, or lower oil prices alone do **not** qualify.
