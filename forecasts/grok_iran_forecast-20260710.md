**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, multi-domain):

Functional normality requires **all** of the following to hold sustainably for at least 30 consecutive days without major reversal:

- **Direct military activity**: No US/Israeli/Iranian strikes on each other’s territory/assets or major naval engagements in/near the Gulf; de-escalation of active kinetic operations.
- **Proxy activity**: Significant restraint by Iran-backed groups (e.g., Houthis, Hezbollah remnants, Iraqi militias) with no sustained attacks on shipping, Gulf states, or US assets.
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz and nearby waters**: No routine attacks, mining, or credible threats forcing rerouting; international (not Iran-dictated) traffic separation schemes operational with freedom of navigation.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Vessel traffic and oil/LNG throughput returning to ≥80% of pre-war baselines (~20-21 million barrels/day equivalent capacity), with consistent daily transits.
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Premiums normalized to low single-digit multiples of pre-war levels (not 1000x+ surges); standard commercial coverage widely available without government backstops or selective IRGC “permits.”
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Uninterrupted flows from Gulf producers (Saudi, UAE, Iraq, Qatar, Iran) without force majeure declarations; no major stranded cargoes or refinery/port shutdowns.
- **Energy market stress and volatility**: Brent/WTI prices and implied volatility returning to pre-war ranges (±20% of ~$70-80/bbl baseline) with stable forward curves; no rationing or acute shortages in Asia/Europe.
- **Force posture of major actors**: Drawdown of US naval/air assets from heightened alert; Iran IRGC Navy not dominating chokepoint enforcement; Gulf states resuming normal operations.
- **Absence of major new escalation triggers**: No assassinations, major proxy surges, nuclear breakthroughs/sabotage, or direct threats to close Hormuz that reignite crisis.

A pause or fragile ceasefire does **not** equal normality. Temporary price drops or selective transits do not suffice.
