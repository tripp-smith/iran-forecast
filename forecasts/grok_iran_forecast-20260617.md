**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, observable, multi-domain):

Functional normality means all of the following sustained for at least 30 consecutive days without major reversal:

- **Direct military activity**: No strikes, drone/missile exchanges, or naval clashes between Iran/US/Israel or their forces in/near the Gulf.
- **Proxy activity**: No significant Hezbollah/Houthi/PMF attacks on shipping, Gulf states, or Israel attributable to Iranian direction.
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz and nearby waters**: Safe, routine transits without credible threats, mine risks, or IRGC interference; established safe corridors with international verification.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Vessel traffic and tonnage (oil/LNG/tankers/containers) returning to ≥80% of pre-war averages (pre-Feb 2026 baseline ~60+ transits/day, ~20 mb/d oil equivalent).
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Standard commercial war-risk premiums near pre-war levels (e.g., <<1% of hull value per transit); full private market availability without government backstops or exclusions for the Gulf.
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Gulf producers (Saudi, UAE, Iraq, Qatar, Iran, Kuwait) exporting at near-capacity with minimal force majeure; no major stranded cargoes or production shut-ins due to transit issues.
- **Energy market stress and volatility**: Brent/WTI prices and implied volatility returning to stable pre-war ranges (absent unrelated shocks); no sustained premiums from Gulf risk.
- **Force posture of major actors**: US/coalition drawdown of surge assets (e.g., extra carriers, mine countermeasures) toward pre-crisis levels; Iranian naval/IRGC forces in defensive, non-disruptive posture.
- **Absence of major new escalation triggers**: No disputes over tolls/sovereignty leading to closures, nuclear breakout signals, or proxy surges; credible implementation of the June 2026 MOU.

A temporary pause, partial traffic, or price dip alone does not qualify.
