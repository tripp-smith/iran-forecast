**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, observable, multi-domain):

Functional normality means a sustained state where:

- **Direct military activity**: No US/Israeli/Gulf strikes on Iranian targets or vice versa (missiles, drones, naval) for at least 30 consecutive days; no declared blockades.
- **Proxy activity**: Significant restraint by Iran-backed groups (Houthis, Hezbollah remnants, Iraqi militias) with no major attacks on shipping, Gulf states, or Israel for 30+ days.
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz/nearby waters**: No successful or attempted attacks on commercial vessels for 30+ days; US/Gulf naval presence supports (not contested) safe passage; mine clearance complete or no longer a barrier.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Daily vessel transits approach or exceed ~70-80% of pre-war baseline (~100-140 vessels/day historically); no widespread rerouting or stranding.
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Premiums return to low single-digit multiples of pre-war levels (e.g., <1% of hull value for 7-day policies vs. current 1-5%+ or cancellations); major insurers resume full coverage without government backstops.
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Iranian, Saudi, Qatari, UAE exports flow at near pre-war volumes without major interruptions; no sustained chokepoint-induced global shortages.
- **Energy market stress and volatility**: Brent/WTI volatility normalizes (e.g., no repeated spikes >$10-15/bbl on Gulf news); no fuel shortages reported in Asia/Europe.
- **Force posture of major actors**: US/Iranian naval/air forces de-escalate to routine patrols; no heightened alerts or mass mobilizations.
- **Absence of major new escalation triggers**: No major incidents (e.g., high-casualty attacks, nuclear-related moves, leadership assassinations) for the period.

Temporary pauses, lower prices alone, or battlefield lulls do not qualify.
