**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, multi-domain):

- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz and nearby waters**: No credible threats/attacks on commercial vessels; US/Iranian forces not actively interdicting or coercing traffic; mine/ drone risks neutralized or negligible.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Daily transits approaching or exceeding ~50-70% of pre-war norms (~60-100+ vessels/day baseline, with full tanker flows); no widespread rerouting or avoidance.
