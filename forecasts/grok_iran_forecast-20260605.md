**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, observable, multi-domain):

Functional normality is achieved when **all** of the following hold simultaneously for a sustained period (at least 7-14 consecutive days, with no immediate reversal signals):

- **Direct military activity**: No kinetic strikes by US/Israel/Iran or their forces in/near the Gulf or on each other’s assets; no active US naval blockade of Iranian ports.
- **Proxy activity**: Significant restraint by Iran-backed groups (e.g., Houthis, Hezbollah remnants, PMF) in attacks threatening Gulf shipping/infrastructure; no major new activations.
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz/nearby waters**: Freedom of navigation without routine harassment, mining, or attacks; IRGC “management”/permission schemes ended or neutralized; US/coalition escorts no longer required for most traffic.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Daily vessel transits through Hormuz approaching or exceeding ~60-80% of pre-war baseline (pre-Feb 2026 ~60+/day; current ~10-18/day as of early June 2026).<grok:render card_id="8f6531" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">53</argument></grok:render>
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Premiums return to low single-digit multiples of pre-war levels (e.g., <<1% of hull value per transit); broad private market coverage available without government backstops.
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Gulf exports (via Hormuz) recovering to ~70-80%+ of pre-war volumes (~15-20 mb/d oil equivalent); no major force majeure declarations; Asian/European buyers resuming normal offtake without rerouting premiums.
- **Energy market stress/volatility**: Brent and regional benchmarks stable with implied volatility normalized (no sustained crisis premia); no ongoing global supply shock equivalent to the largest historical disruption.
- **Force posture of major actors**: US/coalition drawdown from heightened alert/blockade; Iranian naval/IRGC forces not postured for closure or harassment; Gulf states resuming normal port/refinery ops.
- **Absence of major new escalation triggers**: No imminent threats to nuclear sites, leadership, or critical infrastructure; diplomatic channels active without brinkmanship over Hormuz/nuclear issues.

A temporary pause, partial traffic under IRGC rules, subsidized insurance, or lower oil prices alone do **not** qualify.
