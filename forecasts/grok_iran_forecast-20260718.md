**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, observable, multi-domain): 

Functional normality means sustained, low-risk commercial operations without significant military interference or economic distortion across these indicators:
- **Direct military activity**: No routine strikes by US/Iran or allies in/near the Strait or Gulf waters; sporadic incidents below a threshold that deters shipping (e.g., <1-2 confirmed attacks per month on commercial vessels).
- **Proxy activity**: Restrained or deterred actions by Houthis, Hezbollah remnants, or others that do not materially threaten Gulf shipping or energy infrastructure.
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz/nearby waters**: Safe transit via standard routes without mandatory IRGC coordination, mine threats, or frequent warnings; US/Gulf naval presence supports rather than enforces amid active conflict.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Vessel traffic approaching ~80-100+ per day (near pre-war ~120-130 baseline), with consistent oil/LNG flows.
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Premiums return to low single-digit basis points (near pre-war ~0.1-0.25% of hull value per transit); broad availability without special consortia or government backstops; no routine cancellations or 7-day notices dominating decisions.<grok:render card_id="34ef47" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">25</argument></grok:render><grok:render card_id="af63b5" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">27</argument></grok:render>
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Flows at/near pre-war volumes (~20M bpd oil equivalent through Hormuz) without major backlogs or forced rerouting; stable production/export from key terminals.
- **Energy market stress/volatility**: Brent/WTI volatility and risk premia normalized (e.g., no sustained geopolitical spikes >$5-10/bbl); Asian/European buyers resuming standard contracting without inventory hoarding.
- **Force posture of major actors**: US drawdown or stabilization of elevated presence (e.g., reduced carrier groups, <50k troops focused on deterrence rather than active strikes); Iran refrains from asserting control via tolls/mines/strikes.
- **Absence of major new escalation triggers**: No nuclear breakout, major proxy surges, Israeli strikes on Iranian territory, or Gulf state basing disputes that restart high-intensity cycles.
