**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, observable, multi-domain):

Functional normality means **all** of the following sustained for at least 14 consecutive days without major reversal:
- **Direct military activity**: No US/Iran/Israel strikes or significant kinetic exchanges in/near the Gulf; no active US blockade of Iranian ports.
- **Proxy activity**: No major activations or escalations by Iran-backed groups (e.g., Houthis, Hezbollah remnants, Iraqi militias) targeting Gulf shipping, infrastructure, or US/GCC assets.
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz and nearby waters**: Safe commercial transit via multiple routes without routine attacks, credible threats, or forced IRGC coordination; mine risks cleared or negligible.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Daily transits approaching pre-war norms (~100-130+ vessels/day, including substantial non-Iranian tankers/LNG carriers with AIS on); backlog cleared.
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Hull war premiums near pre-war baselines (~0.25% or less of hull value); full commercial availability without special consortia or government backstops; no mandatory Iranian/PGSA insurance mandates.
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Gulf exports (Saudi, UAE, Iraq, Qatar, Iran) at or near pre-war levels (~18-20+ mbpd equivalent combined); no sustained disruptions to loading/ports.
- **Energy market stress and volatility**: Brent/WTI in stable range without geopolitical premium spikes (>10-15% sustained moves tied to Gulf risks); no major buyer hoarding or rerouting.
- **Force posture of major actors**: US/CENTCOM drawdown from heightened alert; Iranian IRGC Navy not actively enforcing control or attacking deviating vessels; GCC states not in emergency mode.
