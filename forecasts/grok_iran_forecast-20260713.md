**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, multi-domain):<grok:render card_id="23796a" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">18</argument></grok:render><grok:render card_id="44cb65" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">64</argument></grok:render>

- **Direct military activity**: No significant US/Israeli strikes on Iranian targets or Iranian missile/drone attacks on US/Gulf/Israeli assets for at least 30 consecutive days.
- **Proxy activity**: No major proxy (e.g., Houthis, Hezbollah remnants, Iraqi militias) attacks on shipping, Gulf infrastructure, or Israel/US forces exceeding sporadic incidents.
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz/nearby waters**: Sustained safe transit without routine attacks, mine threats, or forced rerouting; daily transits approaching pre-war norms (~100+ vessels/day).
- **Commercial transit levels**: Vessel traffic and DWT throughput at ≥80% of pre-war averages for 4+ weeks; minimal vessels waiting/anchored due to risk.
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Premiums back to low single-digit multiples of pre-war (not 10x–4000x spikes); broad private coverage availability without government backstops.
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Gulf exports (via Hormuz) at ≥80% of pre-war volumes (~15-20 mb/d oil equivalent) with stable flows; no widespread force majeure.
- **Energy market stress/volatility**: Brent volatility normalized (no sustained >$10-15/bbl swings from Gulf news); no acute supply shocks.
- **Force posture of major actors**: US naval presence de-escalated from blockade/high-alert; Iranian IRGC navy not actively harassing; Gulf states resuming normal operations.
- **Absence of major new escalation triggers**: No unresolved nuclear crises, leadership assassinations, or major proxy flare-ups driving renewed closure/threats.
