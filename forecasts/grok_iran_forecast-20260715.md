**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, observable, multi-domain):

Functional normality requires **all** of the following to hold simultaneously and sustainably (not just a temporary lull):

- **Direct military activity**: No significant US/Israeli strikes on Iranian territory or IRGC assets; no major Iranian missile/drone attacks on Gulf shipping, US bases, or allies.
- **Proxy activity**: Restraint or major de-escalation by Hezbollah, Houthis, Iraqi PMF, etc., with no sustained attacks disrupting Gulf or Red Sea shipping/energy infrastructure.
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz and nearby waters**: No mine-laying, seizures, or attacks on commercial vessels; US naval presence supports rather than blocks routine transit; IRGC "closure" declarations ended.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Vessel traffic and tanker transits through Hormuz return to ≥80% of pre-war baselines (pre-Feb 2026 levels of ~20+ million bpd oil equivalent throughput).
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Private market premiums return to low single-digit multiples of pre-war levels (e.g., <1% of hull value for 7-day policies vs. current 1-5%+ or higher); broad availability without government backstops dominating.
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Iranian, Saudi, UAE, Iraqi, etc., exports flowing at near-capacity with minimal disruptions; no major floating storage buildup or rerouting dependency.
- **Energy market stress and volatility**: Brent/WTI volatility normalizes (e.g., no sustained spikes from chokepoint threats); refining margins and crack spreads return toward historical averages.
- **Force posture of major actors**: Drawdown of US naval/air assets from heightened alert; Iranian IRGC naval forces not in aggressive forward deployment; Gulf states resuming normal port/refinery ops.
- **Absence of major new escalation triggers**: No nuclear breakout signals, major proxy flare-ups, or regime-threatening internal instability driving renewed closure risks.

A battlefield pause or temporary convoy system does not qualify. Insurance normalization and commercial confidence are key gating factors beyond military quiet.
