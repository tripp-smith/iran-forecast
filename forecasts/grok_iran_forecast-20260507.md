**Operational Definition of “Return to Functional Normality”** (measurable, observable, multi-domain; a temporary ceasefire or battlefield pause does *not* suffice):

- **Direct military activity**: No sustained US/Israeli strikes on Iranian territory or Iranian missile/drone attacks on US/Israeli/Gulf state targets exceeding sporadic incidents (<5 significant events/month).
- **Proxy activity**: Restraint by Hezbollah, Houthis, PMF, etc., with attacks on shipping/Gulf infrastructure reduced to pre-2025 baseline levels.
- **Shipping security in Strait of Hormuz/nearby waters**: Safe commercial transit without routine attacks, seizures, or credible threats; US/Iranian naval forces not in active escort/confrontation mode.
- **Commercial transit levels**: Vessel traffic (tankers, containers) through Hormuz at ≥70-80% of pre-war (2025) averages; no widespread rerouting around Africa.
- **War-risk insurance conditions**: Premiums back to <0.5% of hull value (from current spikes of 1-10%); full private market coverage available without government backstops.
- **Oil and LNG export continuity**: Gulf exports (Saudi, UAE, Iraq, Qatar, Iran) at ≥85% of pre-war volumes; Iranian exports normalized subject to sanctions.
- **Energy market stress/volatility**: Brent/WTI volatility index normalized (e.g., <30-day realized vol ~15-20%); no sustained risk premium (> $10-15/bbl geopolitical adder).
- **Force posture of major actors**: US naval presence at routine levels (not surge escort/blockade); Iranian IRGC Navy not in heightened harassment posture; Gulf states de-alerted.
- **Absence of major new escalation triggers**: No nuclear breakout signals, leadership crises, or major proxy flare-ups; credible diplomatic track toward longer-term de-escalation.
